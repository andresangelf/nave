## Bloque base (pegar al inicio de cada prompt)

> Photorealistic product photography of a café barista uniform inspired by astronaut flight-training suits, for "Nave Café", a specialty coffee and coworking space in San Salvador. Follow the attached reference sheets exactly for cut, color placement and details. Palette: off-white (#F7F7F5) cotton twill body, light grey (#D9DDDE) shoulder yoke and chest pockets, mid grey (#B8BFC1) apron, dark grey (#4A5558) trousers and name tape, orange-red (#E55C41) used only for small details (sleeve cuffs, zipper pull, hem stripe, apron strap pull, dot on patches). Minimal El Salvador flag patch on the left sleeve: three horizontal stripes blue (#0047AB), white, blue, no coat of arms. Clean, functional, NASA ground-crew aesthetic, not costume-like, no helmets, no spacesuit bulk. Real fabric texture, visible stitching, velcro patches, metal zipper. Natural soft light, shallow depth of field, 35 mm look with subtle film grain. No extra logos, no invented text.
